# Entry Screening

What makes a candidate worth surfacing in the morning digest.

- **Fundamental quality:** reasonable valuation vs peers/history, healthy margins,
  manageable debt, positive or improving growth.
- **Technical posture:** constructive trend (above rising 50/200-day MA), not extremely
  overbought (RSI below `rsi_overbought`).
- **Sentiment:** improving news tone or positive analyst revisions (leading indicator).
- **Macro fit:** sector tailwinds, not fighting rates/commodity headwinds.
- **Catalyst:** an upcoming, identifiable driver (earnings, product, guidance).

Rank candidates with the `signal_weights` composite. Prefer ideas with a clear thesis you
can later test for "thesis broken." Index/ETF ideas should favor low expense ratios and
durable long-term returns.
