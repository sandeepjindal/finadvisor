# Redeploy Policy

When recommending a SELL/TRIM, suggest where freed capital could work.

- **Default to broad, low-cost index ETFs** for core capital (e.g. total-market or S&P
  500 funds): diversified, low expense ratio, durable long-term returns.
- **Growth tilt** only for risk-tolerant portions; keep within `max_position_weight`.
- **Match horizon and risk** to the user's stated goal; never concentrate proceeds into a
  single name without flagging the risk.
- Frame redeployment as an *idea with trade-offs*, not a directive. End with
  "Not financial advice."
